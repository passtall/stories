# Current mystery stories

This directory intentionally contains only the current five-story batch. Earlier narratives were deleted from the repository at the user's request, but their case identifiers remain in `../already-told-stories.md` so they will not be selected again.

These five were chosen under a stricter criterion: the event should sound implausible or invented **and** have unusually strong evidence behind it, such as official records, declassified documents, institutional archives, physical wreck evidence, or contemporary video.

| # | Story | Why it qualifies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Die Hand im versunkenen Schiff](01-the-hand-in-the-wreck.md) | A body-recovery diver reaches for a hand in a tugboat on the seabed, and the hand grabs him back. The rescue exists on video. |
| 02 | [Das Schiff, das keine Steine suchte](02-the-ship-that-was-not-mining.md) | The CIA secretly builds a Howard Hughes “mining” ship to lift a Soviet nuclear-missile submarine from roughly five kilometres below the Pacific. The operation is declassified. |
| 03 | [42 Minuten für einen Baum](03-forty-two-minutes-for-a-tree.md) | After two officers are killed during tree trimming in the Korean DMZ, the return tree-cutting mission is backed by bombers, fighters, artillery, special forces, helicopters and an aircraft carrier. U.S. Army history documents it. |
| 04 | [Der Mann, der ohne Fallschirm sprang](04-the-man-who-jumped-without-a-parachute.md) | A Lancaster rear gunner falls roughly 18,000 feet without a parachute and survives. German captors investigate the claim and corroborate it; RAF Museum archives preserve the evidence. |
| 05 | [Der Schalter im Feld](05-the-switch-in-the-field.md) | A B-52 breaks apart over North Carolina and drops two thermonuclear bombs. Declassified Sandia analysis shows one weapon moved alarmingly far through its arming sequence. |

## Editorial rule

The blacklist, not this directory, is the continuity record. Deleting a story file does **not** make that case eligible again.

Future stories should continue to use the long-form German style defined in `../agents.md`, with a `## Quellen und Beleglage` section that distinguishes well-documented facts from later reconstruction or disputed details.
